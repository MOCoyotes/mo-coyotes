Hi, I'm Coyote

MO Coyotes (Missouri Coyotes) is a blend of thrifted, altered, and handmade. I'm a STL Neurodivergent Leo who wanders in the land of textiles.

Specialties include sewing, knitting, weaving, crocheting, felting, fashion & Barbies. You will mostly find me in my studio with Ca$h at Node 0.

You can shop in person at Union Studio, check my Etsy for other pieces, or find thrifted items in our eBay shop. Check out Coyote in Barbie Land on YouTube.
